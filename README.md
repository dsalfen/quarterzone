# QUARTERZONE

A two-player online quarter-flick game with a Battlezone-style vector look. Play at https://dsalfen.github.io/quarterzone/

**v2 — the configuration version**
- Settings for rules, table & setup, physics, and look & sound
- Built-in and custom presets, saved on your device
- Share your house rules as a link
- Online games use the host's rules; look & sound stay personal
- Generated vaporwave / synthwave soundtrack (no audio files)
