RULES
  • Three quarters start near your edge. Drag any quarter back and release to flick it.
  • Every flick must pass between the other two quarters.
  • Miss the gap and your turn ends.
  • Keep flicking toward the far edge. A good pass that ends past the dashed line scores the zone points; one hanging over the edge scores the hanger points.
  • A quarter falling off ends the turn. Scoring ends your turn.
  • Point values, table size, physics and more can be changed in SETTINGS. Online, the host's rules are used.
