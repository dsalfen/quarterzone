  • Three quarters start near your edge. Drag any quarter back and release to flick it.
  • Every flick must pass between the other two quarters. Bumping them is fine.
  • Miss the gap and your turn ends with 0.
  • Keep flicking toward the far edge. A good pass that ends:
  •   within one quarter-length of the edge (past the dashed line): 1 point
  •   hanging over the edge: 5 points
  •   off the table: 0. Any quarter falling off ends the turn.
  • Scoring ends your turn. First to 15 wins.
